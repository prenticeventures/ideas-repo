## 2. Embroidery & Sewing on Demand

### 2.1 What Exists Today

Embroidery-on-demand (EOD) is real and growing — but almost entirely focused on apparel and accessories.

| Platform | Embroidery products | Techniques | Notes |
|----------|--------------------|-----------|-|
| Printful | 192 products | Flat, 3D puff, satin stitch, tatami fill, run stitch | Best quality control; priciest |
| Printify | 80+ products | Flat embroidery via third-party providers | Most cost-competitive |
| Gelato | Select products | Flat embroidery | Polos, hoodies, hats, beanies only |
| Apliiq | Shirts, hoodies, hats | Flat + custom placement | Bulk discounts; free shipping over $100 US |

### 2.2 Embroidered Wall Art — Does It Exist as POD?

**Short answer: No.** No mainstream POD vendor supports automated embroidered wall art, framed fabric, or textile canvas. The market is either (a) handmade by artisans on Etsy at high prices, or (b) mass-produced overseas and sold pre-made. Neither is compatible with a fully automated, passive model today.

### 2.3 Creative Angles Worth Exploring

- **Embroidery aesthetic prints:** Design your topo maps with a visual style that mimics embroidery thread, cross-stitch patterns, or needlepoint. Print on canvas via standard POD. Same automated workflow, distinctive visual identity. Most practical near-term option.
- **Throw pillows as a bridge product:** Embroidered throw pillows and blankets do exist on Printify and Printful. A topo map pillow alongside a canvas print could be a differentiated accessory.
- **Watch this space:** POD for home textiles is evolving rapidly. If embroidered wall art becomes automated in the next 1–2 years, you'd be well-positioned to add it.

> **Recommendation:** Skip physical embroidery for launch. Focus on canvas prints, which are a mature, automated, high-margin category. Revisit embroidery in 12–18 months, or explore the "embroidery-aesthetic print" angle as a visual differentiator that costs nothing extra to produce.

- **Market size:** 65M+ US homeowners; 44M renter households. US home décor market: $150B+.

## 5. Competitive Landscape

### 5.1 Market Summary

The custom map art market divides into (a) functional/utilitarian maps (USGS-derived, accurate but not art-forward) and (b) stylized city/street map art (aesthetically driven but not topographic). Your product — visually rich artistic topo maps with full color customization — occupies a gap that no current competitor squarely fills.

### 5.2 Key Competitors

**Mapiful — mapiful.com**
The most prominent brand in custom map art. Primarily sells street map art (road/block layouts of cities) — not topographic maps. Self-serve editor, museum-grade paper. Free shipping over $59.
- Small poster (11×17"): $39 • Medium (18×24"): $59 • Large (24×36"): $79
- **Key limitation:** Street maps only. No topographic rendering. No wilderness, mountain, or off-grid locations.

**Topographia Design — topographiadesign.com**
Your most direct competitor. Minimalist contour line style on fine art paper. Custom location for +$40.
- Canvas + wood frame: $76–$194 depending on size.
- **Key limitation:** Minimalist style only — close to a technical drawing. No artistic color schemes. Your visual richness is a meaningful differentiator.

### 6.3 Phase 3: The Customer Configurator (Year 2+)

This is your most important long-term infrastructure investment.

- **Option A (Recommended):** Mapbox-Based Custom Configurator. Mapbox GL JS + your existing render engine + Shopify Storefront API. Mapbox free tier: 50,000 map loads/month. Build cost: $2,000–$6,000 for a freelance frontend developer.
- **Option B (Zero-cost interim):** Manual intake flow — "message me your coordinates and preferred color." Validates custom demand before building automation.
- **Option C (No-code bridge):** Zakeke or Customily on Shopify. $30–60/mo. Best as a stopgap.

### 6.4 Legal & Operational Notes

- **Elevation data:** USGS, SRTM, and OpenTopoData datasets are public domain — no licensing fees. Mapbox allows prints for commercial use on standard plans.
- **Etsy production partner:** Etsy requires POD partner disclosure. Standard and expected by buyers.
- **Copyright:** Your artistic rendering of geographic data is an original work you own. Add "© [Brand] 2026" to renders. Register with US Copyright Office (~$65) once you have a meaningful portfolio.
- **Business entity:** An LLC is worth forming at ~$1,000+/month. $50–$500 to register depending on state.
- **Sales tax:** Etsy and Shopify both collect and remit marketplace facilitator sales tax automatically in most US states.
