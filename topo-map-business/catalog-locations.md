# Topo Map Art — Initial Product Portfolio: 25 Locations
*Finalized: March 4, 2026 | Replaces earlier draft research*

---

## On Portfolio Size

20–30 locations does not crowd the Etsy offering. Each listing is an independent search entry point — buyers find listings through search, not by browsing a 30-item shop menu. A deep, coherent catalog (same style, same series, many locations) actually builds credibility. Muir Way operates 100+ topo products successfully. The only crowding risk is mixing incompatible *product types*, not volume.

**Ski destinations included:** ~6 of 25 locations are ski-primary, with 1 (Jackson Hole) being a ski/NP crossover. All ski destinations are also legitimate summer outdoor destinations, so listings work year-round.

## On the Wedding / Anniversary Segment

Static catalog locations are not the right vehicle for this segment — the value is entirely in the "our location" custom angle. Handle this with:

1. **Now (Etsy phase):** A single "Custom Map — Any Location Worldwide" listing. Buyer submits coordinates in the order notes. Zero additional infrastructure, passive income from this segment.
2. **Later (Shopify phase):** Full configurator where buyers enter coordinates, choose color scheme, preview, and purchase. This is the right time to invest in the segment properly.
