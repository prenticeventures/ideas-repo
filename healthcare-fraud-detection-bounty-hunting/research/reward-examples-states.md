# Public evidence of payments under state Medicaid tip-reward programs

## Finding

Among the six state routes reviewed, Florida is the only one for which official AHCA report text located through the official-site search index reports money paid under the Medicaid tip-reward law. The underlying report files could not be retrieved from the currently listed URLs, so these totals are indexed-text evidence rather than a direct review of the PDF pages. Florida's annual report gives an aggregate fiscal-year total, not the payee, individual award, tip, or case. No case-specific informant payment was verified in the official sources reviewed for any of the six states.

“Not located” means the bounded review did not find a public award record. It does not establish that the state made no awards. A statute authorizing a payment, a budget or fund balance, a civil or criminal recovery, and a report that a tip led to a case are not evidence by themselves that an informant received a reward.

## Search limits

This was a public-record review, not a request to the agencies. I checked the cited statutes and official AG, Medicaid agency, annual-report, case-announcement, budget, or rulemaking materials where available. The Arkansas AG announcement page was accessed and describes a tip-to-case lead, but no reward. Florida’s report PDF links currently do not resolve, so the payment totals are supported by official-site indexed text rather than retrieved report files. The current Louisiana administrative-code document was downloaded and searched for the tip-reward section. No agencies, tipsters, or other people were contacted. Award records may be confidential, omitted from public reports, or described under a different label. In particular, settlement shares under a state or federal false-claims lawsuit are not counted here as tip-reward-program payments.
