# Healthcare fraud detection and reward programs

Research date: October 25, 2026

[Read the full research report](REPORT.md) or [the executive summary](DECISION-MEMO.md).

Standalone browser versions: [full research report](Healthcare-Fraud-Research.html) and [WHIP deep dive](WHIP-Research.html).

## Assessment and limits

The evidence supports conditional feasibility for specialized investigation work. Reliable income from automated public-data tips is not established. The report distinguishes a billing signal from corroborated evidence and a qualifying payment outcome.

The state index covers all 50 states and D.C. at a screening level. Insurer checks concern eight specified pages/products. Detailed state data access was examined selectively. Source blocks, provisional programs, missing clinical records and unverified actual award receipts are identified in the research.
